§02 · Background

The short version.

I'm a Singapore-based product designer with over a decade of experience across mobility tech, enterprise software, and multimedia design. I don't specialise in one layer of the stack — I've designed booking flows, built the visual language for a company's public face, and sat with the operators using the tools I ship. I like moving between those altitudes.

For the past six years I've been at SWAT Mobility, now as Senior Product Designer. My work spans the passenger-facing app people use to book rides, the company website, and the Route Generator — the tool operators rely on to plan and optimise routes at scale. It's taught me to design for two very different audiences at once: the rider who wants three taps, and the operator who lives in the tool all day.

Before that, I spent over three years at Multimedia People (MMP Singapore) as a Multimedia Designer, which turned out to be an unusually good foundation for a generalist. Pitch visuals and wireframes, UI and motion design in After Effects, some front-end build work, even physical exhibition design — elevation drawings, wall prints, and the interior layout for a gallery's audio guide. Fewer boundaries between disciplines than most design jobs allow, and I still think that's the right way to learn.

I hold a first-class honours BA in Communication Design from the Singapore Institute of Technology, and a Diploma in Interaction Design from Nanyang Polytechnic before that.

Off screen, I lead and design craft activities for four- and five-year-olds at children's ministry — which is its own crash course in designing for an audience with a two-minute attention span and very strong opinions. I'm also usually mid-kdrama for the plot twists, chasing down a good sticker at art fairs, and slowly running out of room to store the collection.
